# Business Findings & Insights

## Project Overview

This analysis explores sales performance, revenue realization, customer segments, discounting, delivery outcomes, and product category profitability using an Excel-based reporting workbook.

## 1. Revenue Realization

The dataset contains 138,116 orders with a total order value of ₹157.14 million. Realized revenue was ₹127.91 million, representing approximately 81.40% of total order value.

The remaining value is associated with cancelled, pending, and returned orders.

**Business implication:** Total order value alone does not provide a complete picture of sales performance. Management should monitor order completion and order status alongside revenue.

## 2. Annual Revenue Trends

Annual realized revenue remained relatively stable over the five-year period:

- 2021: ₹25.99 million
- 2022: ₹25.46 million
- 2023: ₹25.66 million
- 2024: ₹25.45 million
- 2025: ₹25.34 million

Revenue declined in 2022, recovered modestly in 2023, and decreased slightly in both 2024 and 2025.

**Business implication:** The business did not demonstrate sustained annual revenue growth during this period. Further analysis could investigate changes in order volume, average order value, and product performance.

## 3. Customer Segment Profitability

The segment analysis shows differences in average discounts and margins:

| Customer Segment | Average Discount | Margin |
|---|---:|---:|
| Business | 13.78% | 37.19% |
| Consumer | 13.70% | 37.49% |
| Premium | 17.67% | 33.20% |
| VIP | 17.78% | 33.29% |

Premium and VIP segments had higher average discounts and lower margins than Business and Consumer segments.

**Business implication:** Review discounting and profitability by customer segment before changing pricing or promotional strategies. The observed relationship does not establish that higher discounts caused lower margins.

## 4. Delivery Performance and Customer Ratings

Average customer ratings varied by delivery outcome:

| Delivery Outcome | Average Rating |
|---|---:|
| Early | 4.02 |
| On Time | 3.73 |
| Delayed | 3.22 |

Delayed orders had the lowest average customer rating, while early deliveries had the highest.

**Business implication:** Investigating delivery delays may help identify opportunities to improve customer experience.

## 5. Product Category Contribution and Margin

The product-level category analysis showed that Electronics contributed approximately 21.65% of total product line value, with a category margin of approximately 27.41%.

Grocery contributed approximately 1.46% of product line value and had a category margin of approximately 55.22%.

**Business implication:** High sales contribution and high margin are not necessarily found in the same category. Management could investigate the cost structure, pricing, and growth opportunities of categories with different sales and margin profiles.


## Overall Recommendations

1. Monitor order completion and realized revenue alongside total order value.
2. Investigate the drivers of relatively flat annual revenue.
3. Review segment-level discounting and profitability together.
4. Examine the causes and business impact of delivery delays.
5. Compare category sales contribution with profitability when evaluating product opportunities.

## Limitations

These findings are descriptive and depend on the dataset and metric definitions used in the workbook. Figures may also differ when interactive filters are applied.
